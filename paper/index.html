Ranking content by material decline opportunity using historical search-performance signals, content characteristics, and leakage-aware validation.

Abstract

From raw signals to a prioritized review queue

The project develops a machine-learning workflow that ranks content items for potential refresh review. Random Forest and HistGradientBoosting were evaluated alongside a most-frequent baseline using a held-out, client-group-aware test split. Random Forest achieved an F1-score of 0.5734 and ROC-AUC of 0.6608, while HistGradientBoosting achieved the highest ROC-AUC at 0.6717. The Random Forest ranking concentrated observed material decline into a High Opportunity tier with a 74.38% decline rate versus 46.24% in the Lower Opportunity tier. That corresponds to a 1.61× relative lift and a 28.14 percentage-point difference.

Primary research question

Can historical search and engagement signals be used to rank content pages for future refresh review?

Decision supported: prioritize investigation and editorial triage; do not treat the model as a causal or automatic content-refresh decision system.

01 · Problem

Turn search-performance change into a review priority

Why this matters

Large content collections make manual review expensive. The project asks whether historical performance and static content signals can identify a smaller group that deserves earlier investigation.

What the system does

It predicts a material-decline outcome, scores held-out observations with the Random Forest model, and turns those scores into percentile-based review tiers with reason codes and an action playbook.

Public-safe framing

The website intentionally excludes client names, private queries, credentials, raw exports, and identifying URL/content values.

Prediction target

Material decline is defined from the change in impressions between the latest 30-day period and the preceding 30-day period. A material decline is an impression change of -60% or worse. Outcome-period measurements are excluded from the model inputs.

The original is_declining field is constant (True) across the inspected lane and is therefore not used as the final target.

07 · Reason codes & actions

Turn score into an explainable review playbook

Reason codes

Example supporting signals attached to the ranked queue:

LOW_HISTORICAL_IMPRESSIONSLOW_HISTORICAL_CLICKSLOW_HISTORICAL_SESSIONSOLDER_CONTENTDEEP_URL_STRUCTUREMODEL_SCORE_DRIVEN

Reason codes are prioritization context, not causal explanations.

Action playbook

High · Immediate review
Review recent search-performance changes, relevance, freshness, title/keyword alignment, and potential technical or structural issues.
Medium · Scheduled review
Review trends, quality gaps, and comparable content; intervene when additional evidence supports it.
Lower · Monitor
Continue monitoring and avoid unnecessary changes unless new evidence increases the priority.
08 · Limitations

Honest framing matters as much as model score

Dataset limitation

Results describe the available anonymized snapshot and should not automatically be generalized to other search environments.

Prediction limitation

Model performance is moderate rather than perfect; incorrect classifications remain.

Distribution limitation

Future changes in content, demand, user behavior, or search systems can alter model performance.

Causal limitation

Feature importance and predicted risk do not establish that changing a feature will improve search performance.

Ranking limitation

An opportunity score identifies items for review; it does not guarantee future decline or refresh success.

Correct interpretation

Model-based content-review prioritization and decision support.
